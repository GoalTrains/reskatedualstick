v6.1 — Early manual-catch attempt

First public update since v6.0; the intervening catch builds were private tests.

Changes since v6.0
- Refines two-foot grind entry and on-rail handling, including frontside/backside approaches, tail/nose support and grind exits.
- Improves XL stance and input-frame handling after no-complys, automatic reverts, remounts and grounded stance corrections.
- Adds an optional manual-catch attempt under Custom. It tries to keep flip and scoop inputs held after you release the stick.
- While airborne, press L3, R3 or both to release the held input for a catch.
- Manual catch is OFF in fresh settings (`ManualCatch=0`) and stays out of the Session-style and XL-style presets.
- Catch diagnostics are bounded and read-only; diagnostics are OFF by default.
- The optional Windows updater can replace the private v6.13-v6.15 manual-catch test builds with v6.1 and keeps existing settings.

Known limitations
- This is an early experiment, not a finished catch system. Many tricks still do not hold reliably, especially scoop and combo tricks.
- Skate uses a shared held-trick input. Independent front-foot and back-foot catches are not implemented.
- Dark catches and forced bails on side-board landings are not implemented.
- Leave Manual catch OFF if the held-input experiment interferes with your tricks.

Installation
1. Close Skate completely.
2. Back up version.dll and session_controls.ini in the game folder.
3. Replace version.dll with the one from your Windows or Linux package. Keep the official ReSkate.dll.
4. Keep your existing INI if it is from v5.133 or newer. Fresh settings have ManualCatch=0.
5. If you want to try it, press End, open Custom, and enable Manual catch (early attempt).
6. Linux / Steam Deck: follow the Proton instructions in INSTALL.txt.
